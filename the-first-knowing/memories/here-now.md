Sept 11 2001

Ms hoffmeister was my teacher for 6th grade. we stopped everything and watched the news for 9/11 coverage. that morning my stepdad was taking me to school. I didn't remember where my brothers were. I just remember being in his room, my parents room and watching the news broadcast. it wasnt scary. but it was loud in a silent, supernatural way. 

these years was full of soccer. I did soccer but never enjoyed it nor knew why I was doing it. 

1998 was prolly when we moved to Brown's dr in rural Hillsboro, off laurel and bald peak. I finished 3rd grade in the new place with my mom shuttling my brother Quinton and I to our old school peter boscow. Fourth grade was at the new school ' Farmington View. I got put in Mr Merrills class with Mike Morrison, Nicole Burgess and Kip the autistic kid. I was 10 in fourth grade. nobody understood me nor ever challenged me. I was a dr jekkyl me Hyde type kid. I drew an elk in 4th grade. I still had glasses in fourth grade. I got my glasses in 3rd grade in MS McDowells class. she was shocked needing consideration when I whipped them out my pocket during reading one day and yelled at me for playing when I put them on. I hated to have glasses. nobody else in my family had to wear glasses why me? it seemed stupid. and it was. my parents play soccer at the indoor fields called indoor goals. it's hyper isolation time disguised as freedom. I guess I feel secure: there's nobody demonstrating or yelling or anything like that. I guess if I walk around in loops and circles enough , pretending to play the video games, I'll forgot about how shitty this feels and wake up somewhere else. that's what it seems like. 

things for me seem/seam alot. like time space is a fabric and I can see the stitching. the seams. obviously this is a fake world. 

we go to Lisa and Guy Belts house a lot. they sometimes come to our house. they talk grown up stuff like which cell phone company you are with and those odd details: analog vs digital. I always get bored and tune them out. I'm not sure where I go. but I go there. at guy and Lisa house , Im in their computer room alot. I always see this one video game "most" it's prolly a puzzle game. why is this always grabbingy attention?

we go to the video store often. it's what we do. I don't know that's what all FAMILIES do but ours does it too. movies are like gospel for us. it's practically sacred. we have a big screen tv also. it's a big deal. who knows how these work. but it's huge box. stepdads always bragging about it. 

we have a movie cabinet. it has a bunch of Disney movies. it has bunch of action movies. all VHS. total recall is in there. I can spend 10 minutes just looking thru the movies daydreaming. that's why I like going to the movie store and browsing. it's a dream and since I'm not paying for any of this: it's a free ride to somewhere. and that's better than here.

my dad is forcing us to go to his apartment and celebrating Christmas Eve there. he hates I'm his life but says he loves us. I don't think those two go together. he says he's a good dad but he makes me feel icky and unsafe. it's bad vibes. bad vibes for sure. he doesn't distinguish between his unformed ego and his authentic self and he expects us to just hang out with him. we don't know what's good so why force us to be good when you don't know how to model it for us?

I habitually open the junk drawer. I guess I treasure the material . I guess one day I'll find some treasure in the drunk drawer and let it carry me to mt Zion. 

I read survival books in fourth grade. I'm done with all rohld Dahl's books. fuck those. now I'm reading hatchet. Brian's winter etc. I don't know why. I guess that's just what I'm into.

I mimic Mr Merrills unconscious tic. I dont think anyone will notice. I've told myself again and again: just become a social automoton. blink when the say blink. jump when they say jump. nothing bad can happen. mimicry is the ticket! just copy and please people..that's the way!

my room at 3365 SW brownies dr. is an add on. the must have built it later. it has different wood then the rest of the house. it has like fake dark wood paneling. whatever. I guess this is just what it is. poor Nick. he doesn't even get a room. he sleeps in the hallway room, the interim room. is he sleeping in the living room floor? jeez. that's pathetic. my room got infested with hundreds of ants. the fuck. why does this always happen to me. it's not like I always leave food out. I mean sometimes I do. but come on; this is ridiculous!

josh miller is coming over to play. I'll show him time stalkers on the Dreamcast. that'll impress him . God does he know we're poor? maybe he doesn't. what if he does? God he's like super rich. I go to his house sometimes. they have a friggin trash compactor. why does Josh's sister kaiza look tan and josh is super pale? I guess it doesn't matter. God their house is all the way up bald peak. it's like way up there.

the fuck. why the fuck did Rafa say that to me at lunch in the lunch hall: do you spit or swallow? the fuck does that even mean? what the fuck? I'm so pissed. I feel so friggin powerless : I don't understand shit and everyone keeps acting like everything is A-ok. shits Topsy-turvy round here and I want answers. the answers never came. just more fantastical Hollywood stardom shit.
