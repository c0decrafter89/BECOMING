Sept 11 2001

Ms hoffmeister was my teacher for 6th grade. we stopped everything and watched the news for 9/11 coverage. that morning my stepdad was taking me to school. I didn't remember where my brothers were. I just remember being in his room, my parents room and watching the news broadcast. it was scary in a silent, supernatural way.
