# BECOMING: From First Knowing to Forever After
> *A repository for gathering the pieces until they form a map.*

Welcome to the **Becoming** project. This is an autobiographical archive—a quiet, intentional space to gather memories, fragments, and milestones as they come. 

The goal here isn't immediate perfection or a polished final manuscript. It is simply to collect the raw materials with the quiet assurance that, in time, these pieces will be unified into a luminous map of a life well-walked.

---

## 🗺️ The Map of Thresholds

The journey unfolds across distinct seasons of experience, each holding its own lessons, questions, and turning points:

*   **I. The First Knowing** *(Ages 9–12) — Waking up and first impressions*
*   **II. The Storm Years** *(Ages 13–19) — Forging identity under pressure*
*   **III. The Open Field** *(Ages 18–29) — Launching into the wider world*
*   **IV. The True Reckoning** *(Ages 30–45) — Meeting oneself face-to-face*
*   **V. The Deepening** *(Ages 45–65) — Distilling wisdom from experience*
*   **VI. The Long View** *(Ages 65+) — Completing the arc*
*   **VII. What Holds** *(All Ages) — The unchanging anchors*

---

## 🌱 About the Work

Life asks things of us. We change, our surroundings shift, and people move through our stories. Along the way, we discover who we are by uncovering who we are not. 

This repository serves as a container for those realizations. Drop memories, notes, and reflections here knowing they are safe, waiting for the day they are woven together into clarity.

---

## 🧭 Navigating the Archive

*   **`memories/`** — Raw reflections, anecdotes, and sensory fragments from different eras.
*   **`frameworks/`** — Conceptual structures, notes, and maps used to make sense of the past.
*   **`drafts/`** — Prose and writings taking shape over time.
