# Origin Questions
### Simple questions, one-idea answers, for mastering my own history

> Modeled on the pamphlet I found in the BYU library, *The Truth of the Rastafarians*: one question, in simple language, with one answer that holds one idea.

**How to use this:** Read a question, cover the answer, and say it out loud in one sentence. If I can't, I go back to `drafts/autobiography-draft-1.md` for the detail. If an answer is wrong, I fix it here first.

*Draft 1 · October 2026*

## Origins

**1. When and where was I born?**
September 2, 1989, in Portland, Oregon.

**2. Where did my parents meet?**
In Provo, Utah.

**3. Who is my father?**
John, a reserved man from Minnesota and Nebraska who went to BYU and didn't finish.

**4. Who is my mother?**
A Chilean immigrant who moved to Provo from Rexburg.

**5. Why was I born in Oregon instead of Utah?**

**6. How did my mother's family become Mormon?**
Her father brought them into the church in the 1960s.

**7. Who came before me?**
My older brother, Quinton.

**8. Who came after me?**

**9. What is my heritage?**

## Meaning

**120. What is the one thread of my whole life?**
Learning to speak for myself instead of living inside other people's descriptions.

**121. What was my biggest mistake?**
Hiding instead of asking for help.

**122. What has always saved me?**
Someone who opened a door: my mother, a stranger, a trucker.

**123. What do I say to bring myself back?**
"I'm here now. I exist. I continue."

**124. What are my values?**
Honesty, kindness, courage, presence, accountability, gratitude, rest, and community.

**125. What chapter am I in?**
The True Reckoning, doing at thirty-seven the work I skipped at thirteen.
